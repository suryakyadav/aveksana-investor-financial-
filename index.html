Our Bottom-Up Plan

Our financial plan is a bottom-up forecast, built directly from our specific hiring plan and go-to-market strategy across our three revenue streams, anchored in dual markets: South Asia (India, Nepal, Pakistan) for volume and Europe (Nordic, EU) for ACV and defensibility.

1. The Plan (Money Out / Expenses)

  • Raised to date: €75k in Q4 2025 and €100k in August 2026. Cash in bank as of 25 Aug 2026: €82,430.
  • Sprint Grant: A €100k Business Finland Sprint grant applied for, project running Oct 2026 – Mar 2027 (~€70k advance in Q4 2026 + €30k tail on completion in Q1 2027).
  • Seed Round (Multi-Phase): The €1.5M Seed round is raised in two phases — €500k Tranche 1 closing Q4 2026 (alongside the Sprint advance) to begin the first hires, and €1M Tranche 2 closing Q2 2027, once Sprint pilot evidence (project completes Mar 2027) has started converting the pipeline — our ~4.5-month B2B sales cycle puts the first evidence-led institutional close in Q2 2027.
  • Series A: A €3M Series A is targeted for late 2028, the point our own model shows us crossing €500k+ ARR on path to €1M and 15+ university logos.
  • Current Team (The Base): We are currently a team of 5 — 3 founders (CEO, COO, AI Lead) and 2 engineers. "Lights-on" burn is €9,000/mo, held through Q4 2026.
  • Phased Hiring: After Seed T1 closes (Q4 2026), the first 3 hires (2 Senior SW Engineers + 1 AI Engineer) onboard from Q1 2027, taking OpEx to ~€34k/mo. After Seed T2 closes (Q2 2027), the final 4 hires (Sales & Commercial x2, Marketing Ops, UX) onboard, ramping OpEx through 2027. Stabilised burn from Q1 2028: ~€74,000/mo (€70k team & ops + ~€4k dedicated Artha engineering).

2. The Result (Money In / Revenue)

Our growth is driven by two products: the Thesis Support System (TSS) across two geographic markets, with a B2C funnel underneath it, and Artha, our grant-writing engine, now converting its own commercial pipeline.

  • 1. B2C Thesis Support (The Base): Live with ~€300 MRR and 25,000+ active users, modelled to grow ~15% per quarter.
  • 2. B2B Universities (The Scale): Selling our Thesis Support System into universities across South Asia and the EU. ~4.5-month sales cycle. Pricing is regional: €30k ACV in the EU (Helsinki, Metropolia, TAMK and later EU logos), €12k ACV in South Asia (Pune University, Kathmandu University, NUST University — reflecting materially lower institutional IT budgets). No institutional logos are modelled to close before Sprint evidence lands in Q2 2027.
  • 3. B2B Artha (The Second Engine): A specialised grant-writing module, anchored by an active R&D LOI with Tampere University. Two revenue models: licensing (€10k/yr base package — 2 grants + 3 users — with volume discounts for larger accounts) and commission (5–10% of grant funding a startup secures). 2 startups signed on commission (7% and 8%), plus active paid pilots with the City of Vantaa and Business Tampere. ~10 startups directly in pipeline, plus a new campaign with Terkko Health Hub (~100 biotech/medtech startups) as top-of-funnel. Runs mostly on existing team capacity, with a small dedicated engineering slice as it scales.

3. The Grand Finale (The KPIs)

2026 is a stub year (base date 28 Aug 2026) — burn stays lean and TSS institutional revenue is pre-close, while Artha's first commission deals and licensing base are already contributing. The first evidence-led TSS institutional deals land in 2027 as Sprint validation and Seed T1 hires come online, alongside Artha's Terkko-driven pipeline; growth compounds through 2028 into a fully ramped team.

  • Runway — and our nearest-term risk: Modelled cash is tightest right now — it bottoms at ~€58k in Q3 2026, the quarter before Seed T1 + the Sprint advance close. Once T1 lands, cash never falls below ~€582k before Seed Tranche 2 (Q2 2027), then stays above ~€1.04M through the rest of 2028 — Artha's revenue materially cushions the pre-Series-A years — even before the Series A lands late in 2028.
  • 2026 (stub, Q3–Q4): We exit the year at ~€24,800 total ARR (TSS B2C base ~€4,800 + Artha licensing ~€20,000; no institutional logos or commission have landed yet) and ~€15,100 recognised revenue.
  • 2027: Growth to ~€366,000 total ARR (TSS ~€146,000 + Artha ~€220,000) and 7 institutional logos, with Seed Tranche 2 landing Q2 2027 and the first 2 Artha commission deals paying out.
  • 2028: ~€985,000 total ARR (TSS ~€525,000 + Artha ~€460,000) and 26 institutional logos + 46 Artha customers by year end, crossing the Series A bar (€500k+ ARR, 15+ logos) in time for a late-2028 raise.

Risk Assessment & Mitigation

Risk Area Mitigation Strategy
Near-Term Liquidity Tightest Point Is Now, Not Later: Modelled cash bottoms at ~€58k in Q3 2026 — the quarter before Seed Tranche 1 and the Sprint advance close (Q4 2026). Any slippage in that close timeline is the single biggest near-term risk to the plan, ahead of anything in 2027 or 2028.
Long Sales Cycles (Universities) Revenue Diversification: Steady B2C and Artha revenue provide cash flow continuity while the TSS institutional pipeline converts. Artha (departmental budget, plus commission on grant success) offers a faster B2B sales cycle than full university procurements.
AI Commoditization Data Moat: Proprietary workflow data from students → researchers trains our models to be increasingly specific and defensible against generic LLMs.
Hiring Delays Phased Hiring: Seed tranche 1 (Q4 2026) funds 3 critical engineering hires from Q1 2027; tranche 2 (Q2 2027) unlocks the commercial team. Each phase is gated to milestones, reducing recruitment pressure.
Seed Closing Risk Multi-Phase Seed: Splitting into €500k T1 (Q4 '26) and €1M T2 (Q2 '27) reduces single-event risk. Sprint pilot evidence lands between tranches (project completes Mar '27) to strengthen the T2 close.
Sprint Delay Risk Single Point of Dependency: Every institutional close in this model is gated on Sprint evidence (Oct '26–Mar '27) and the 4.5-month sales cycle after it. A delayed Sprint pushes the entire institutional revenue line and the T2/Series A milestones with it.
Artha Commission Concentration Only 2 Deals Quantified: The model books commission from the 2 signed startups only (7%/8%, ~€45k combined, contingent on their Business Finland grants being approved) — the wider 10-startup pipeline and the Terkko Health Hub campaign (~100 biotech/medtech startups) are modelled as licensing customers only, with no commission booked. This likely understates Artha upside, but a lower-than-assumed grant size or approval rate on the 2 signed deals would reduce it.
Series A Risk Multi-Stage De-risking: Sprint funding validates South Asian institutional sales before the Seed; Seed proves traction before Series A. Each round is unlocked by hitting milestones from the previous one, and once Seed T1 closes, Seed + Sprint + Artha revenue fund operations through to the Series A (cash never drops below ~€582k after Q4 2026) — the round is a growth accelerant, not a runway necessity, provided the Q3 2026 liquidity point above is cleared.
