Our GTM & Market Assumptions

Bottom-up forecast grounded in current traction (6 institutional pilots completed, €1.04M won for clients, ~30K users). Revenue comes from two institutional engines, TSS (thesis support for universities) and Artha (grant proposals), sold in Europe first. Adveni (corporate R&D, Q1 2027) is upside.

Proprietary Tech Advantage

We are not a wrapper. Our proprietary model trained on student-to-researcher workflow data delivers high efficiency. With €195k in Cloud Credits, infrastructure costs are effectively zero for Y1–Y3.

The "Lifecycle" Retention Model

We don't just churn users; we graduate them:

  • Student Portal: Bachelors → Masters → PhDs
  • Supervisor Portal: PhDs → Lecturers → Professors
  • Artha: Professors → Researchers
  • Adveni (Q1 2027): Researchers → Industry R&D

Market (Bottom-Up: Buyers × List Price)

Global: €0.9–1.35B a year — universities €323M, grant proposals €359–718M, corporate R&D €190–240M, research centres €33–65M.

Europe, where we sell today: €94–151M a year (TSS €37M + Horizon Europe proposals €57–114M). Tampere-headquartered, GDPR/EU AI Act compliant.

South Asia (upside, not in the plan): TSS pilots starting with Kathmandu University (Nepal), NUST (Pakistan) and Savitribai Phule Pune University (India).

Artha Pricing

  • Universities, public sector, growth-stage startups: €5,000 per proposal in annual volume deals, falling to €2,500 at 50+ a year. Small teams commit up to 5, medium teams 10–15 (~€10k per licence on average).
  • Early-stage startups: €1,500 access fee per application, credited on award, plus a 7–8% or fixed €7.5–60k success fee. Success fees are one-off and excluded from ARR.

Our Investment Thesis

Our Value Proposition: A free individual product (~30K users) creates a qualified, low-cost funnel for paid institutional licences (TSS and Artha), sold in Europe first, with Adveni for corporate R&D as upside.

  • Phase 1 Goal: Tranche 1 (€500k) converts paid pilots into 2–3 university licences and 10+ Artha deals (~€130–145k ARR). By end of 2027 the model reaches a ~€366k run-rate: €333k ARR (7 TSS licences ~€105k + 22 Artha licences ~€228k) plus ~€33k startup success fees, with Adveni launching in Q1 2027.
  • Series A Targets (Late 2028, ~€3M):
    • €500k+ ARR on path to €1M (Proven Scalability) — modelled at €866k recurring ARR (TSS €390k + Artha €476k) plus €119k success fees, a ~€985k run-rate by end of 2028
    • 15+ University Logos (Market Validation) — modelled at 26 TSS licences + 46 Artha licences (72 customers) by end of 2028
    • Lifecycle Upsell Engine (Proven Retention & Expansion)

Risk Assessment & Mitigation

Risk Area Mitigation Strategy
Long Sales Cycles (Universities) Revenue Diversification: Paid pilots (€3–7k) and Artha per-proposal deals bring in cash while TSS licences go through university procurement. Artha sells into departmental, public-sector and startup budgets with a shorter sales cycle.
AI Commoditization Data Moat: Proprietary workflow data from students → researchers trains our models to be increasingly specific and defensible against generic LLMs.
Hiring Delays Phased Hiring: Tranche 1 (Q4 2026) funds 3 engineering hires from Q1 2027; 2 sales and a product designer follow in Q2 2027, and marketing operations in H2 2027. Each phase is gated to milestones, reducing recruitment pressure.
Seed Closing Risk Multi-Phase Seed: A €500k tranche 1 (Q4 '26) funds ~10 months at plan burn; with Business Finland funding it carries the plan to the €1M tranche 2 in Q2–Q3 2027. Tranche 2 follows proof of the first licences. Splitting the round reduces single-event risk.
Business Finland Funding Pending and Spend-Linked: The €100k Sprint is pending (decision 6 Nov). The €125k R&D grant and €125k loan depend on tranche 1 and are paid against project spend, not upfront. The loan is repayable on Business Finland R&D loan terms. Without the Sprint, runway is about one month shorter.
Success-Fee Dependence Kept Out of ARR: ~€119k (12%) of the 2028 run-rate is startup success fees, payable only when a client's grant is awarded. They are treated as one-off and excluded from recurring ARR, so a weak award year affects revenue, not ARR.
Series A Risk Multi-Stage De-risking: Paid pilots validate institutional demand before tranche 2; the seed proves traction before the Series A. Each round is unlocked by hitting milestones from the previous one. Seed plus Business Finland funding covers ≈ 26 months of plan spend at zero revenue (seed alone ≈ 22 months).
