Our Bottom-Up Plan

Our financial plan is a bottom-up forecast, built directly from our specific hiring plan and go-to-market strategy across our three revenue streams, anchored in dual markets: South Asia (India, Nepal, Pakistan) for volume and Europe (Nordic, EU) for ACV and defensibility.

1. The Plan (Money Out / Expenses)

  • Funding Strategy: We start with €200k (€100k bridge + €100k public grant), with a €100k Business Finland Sprint grant applied for to land in H2 2026 (~€70k advance within 30 days of approval + €30k tail on completion).
  • Seed Round (Multi-Phase): The €1.5M Seed round is raised in two phases — €500k tranche 1 closing Q2 2026 to begin partial hiring, and €1M tranche 2 closing Q4 2026 once Sprint pilot evidence has landed. This matches realistic European VC timing (summer slowdown) and discipline expectations.
  • Series A: A €3M Series A is targeted for late 2027, conditional on hitting €500k+ ARR on path to €1M, 15+ university logos, and a proven lifecycle upsell engine.
  • Current Team (The Base): We are currently a team of 5 — 3 founders (CEO, COO, AI Lead) and 2 engineers. "Lights-on" burn is €9,000/mo.
  • Phased Hiring: Q1 2026 lean (€9k/mo). Q2 2026 first 3 hires (engineers + AI engineer) after Seed T1. Q4 2026 final 4 hires (sales, marketing ops, UX) after Seed T2. Stabilised burn from Q1 2027: ~€70,000/mo.

2. The Result (Money In / Revenue)

Our growth is driven by three distinct revenue streams across two geographic markets:

  • 1. B2C Thesis Support (The Base): Live with ~€300 MRR and 25,000+ active users. Linear growth via 3,000–6,000 new users/month.
  • 2. B2B Universities (The Scale): Selling our Thesis Support System into universities across South Asia and the EU. ~4.5-month sales cycle, average ACV €30k. Lead targets: Pune University (India), Kathmandu University (Nepal), NUST University (Pakistan), anchored by an active R&D LOI with Tampere University in the EU.
  • 3. B2B Artha (The Upsell): A specialised grant-writing module. Professors directly requested and paid us to build and pilot this tool. Active paid pilots with the City of Vantaa and Business Tampere. Pricing tiers €2.5k–€20k.

3. The Grand Finale (The KPIs)

The phased Seed structure means a lean H1 2026, ramping team and B2B activity through H2 2026 once Sprint pilots validate institutional demand. Compounding growth begins in 2027 with a fully ramped team.

  • Runway: Multi-phase Seed (€1.5M) + Bridge (€200k) + Sprint grant (€100k) delivers ~24 months of runway. The Series A in late 2027 extends to end of 2028.
  • Year 1 Goal: By end of Month 12, we hit ~€180,000 ARR and €180k full-year revenue (B2C base + 5–6 institutional contracts converted from Sprint-funded pilots).
  • Year 2: Growth to ~€491,000 ARR by end of Month 24, with Series A landing in late 2027.
  • Year 3: ~€1.15M ARR by end of Month 36, on track for Series B fundraising.

Risk Assessment & Mitigation

Risk Area Mitigation Strategy
Long Sales Cycles (Universities) Revenue Diversification: Steady B2C revenue provides cash flow continuity, while Artha (departmental budget) offers a faster B2B sales cycle than full university procurements.
AI Commoditization Data Moat: Proprietary workflow data from students → researchers trains our models to be increasingly specific and defensible against generic LLMs.
Hiring Delays Phased Hiring: Seed tranche 1 funds 3 critical engineering hires; tranche 2 unlocks the commercial team. Each phase is gated to milestones, reducing recruitment pressure.
Seed Closing Risk Multi-Phase Seed: Splitting into €500k T1 (Q2 '26) and €1M T2 (Q4 '26) reduces single-event risk. Sprint pilot evidence between tranches strengthens T2 close.
Series A Risk Multi-Stage De-risking: Sprint funding validates South Asian institutional sales before the Seed; Seed proves traction before Series A. Each round is unlocked by hitting milestones from the previous one.
