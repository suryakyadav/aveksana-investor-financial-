Proprietary Tech Advantage

We are not a wrapper. Our proprietary model trained on student-to-researcher workflow data delivers high efficiency. With €195k in Cloud Credits, infrastructure costs are effectively zero for Y1–Y3.

Risk Assessment & Mitigation

Risk Area Mitigation Strategy
Near-Term Liquidity Tightest Point Is Now, Not Later: Modelled cash bottoms at ~€58k in Q3 2026 — the quarter before Seed Tranche 1 and the Sprint advance close (Q4 2026). Any slippage in that close timeline is the single biggest near-term risk to the plan, ahead of anything in 2027 or 2028.
Long Sales Cycles (Universities) Revenue Diversification: Steady B2C and Artha revenue provide cash flow continuity while the TSS institutional pipeline converts. Artha (departmental budget, plus commission on grant success) offers a faster B2B sales cycle than full university procurements.
AI Commoditization Data Moat: Proprietary workflow data from students → researchers trains our models to be increasingly specific and defensible against generic LLMs.
Hiring Delays Phased Hiring: Seed tranche 1 (Q4 2026) funds 3 critical engineering hires from Q1 2027; tranche 2 (Q2 2027) unlocks the commercial team. Each phase is gated to milestones, reducing recruitment pressure.
Seed Closing Risk Multi-Phase Seed: Splitting into €500k T1 (Q4 '26) and €1M T2 (Q2 '27) reduces single-event risk. Sprint pilot evidence lands between tranches (project completes Mar '27) to strengthen the T2 close.
Sprint Delay Risk Single Point of Dependency: Every institutional close in this model is gated on Sprint evidence (Oct '26–Mar '27) and the 4.5-month sales cycle after it. A delayed Sprint pushes the entire institutional revenue line and the T2/Series A milestones with it.
Artha Commission Concentration Only 2 Deals Quantified: The model books commission from the 2 signed startups only (7%/8%, ~€45k combined, contingent on their Business Finland grants being approved) — the wider 10-startup pipeline and the Terkko Health Hub campaign (~100 biotech/medtech startups) are modelled as licensing customers only, with no commission booked. This likely understates Artha upside, but a lower-than-assumed grant size or approval rate on the 2 signed deals would reduce it.
Series A Risk Multi-Stage De-risking: Sprint funding validates South Asian institutional sales before the Seed; Seed proves traction before Series A. Each round is unlocked by hitting milestones from the previous one, and once Seed T1 closes, Seed + Sprint + Artha revenue fund operations through to the Series A (cash never drops below ~€582k after Q4 2026) — the round is a growth accelerant, not a runway necessity, provided the Q3 2026 liquidity point above is cleared.
