Our Bottom-Up Plan

Our financial plan is a bottom-up forecast, built directly from our specific hiring plan and go-to-market strategy across our three revenue streams, anchored in dual markets: South Asia (India, Nepal, Pakistan) for volume and Europe (Nordic, EU) for ACV and defensibility.

1. The Plan (Money Out / Expenses)

  • Raised to date: €75k in Q4 2025 and €100k in August 2026. Cash in bank as of 25 Aug 2026: €82,430.
  • Sprint Grant: A €100k Business Finland Sprint grant applied for, project running Oct 2026 – Mar 2027 (~€70k advance in Q4 2026 + €30k tail on completion in Q1 2027).
  • Seed Round (Multi-Phase): The €1.5M Seed round is raised in two phases — €500k Tranche 1 closing Q4 2026 (alongside the Sprint advance) to begin the first hires, and €1M Tranche 2 closing Q2 2027, once Sprint pilot evidence (project completes Mar 2027) has started converting the pipeline — our ~4.5-month B2B sales cycle puts the first evidence-led institutional close in Q2 2027.
  • Series A: A €3M Series A is targeted for late 2028, the point our own model shows us crossing €500k+ ARR on path to €1M and 15+ university logos.
  • Current Team (The Base): We are currently a team of 5 — 3 founders (CEO, COO, AI Lead) and 2 engineers. "Lights-on" burn is €9,000/mo, held through Q4 2026.
  • Phased Hiring: After Seed T1 closes (Q4 2026), the first 3 hires (2 Senior SW Engineers + 1 AI Engineer) onboard from Q1 2027, taking OpEx to ~€30k/mo. After Seed T2 closes (Q2 2027), the final 4 hires (Sales & Commercial x2, Marketing Ops, UX) onboard, ramping OpEx through 2027. Stabilised burn from Q1 2028: ~€70,000/mo.

2. The Result (Money In / Revenue)

Our growth is driven by our Thesis Support System (TSS) across two geographic markets, with a B2C funnel underneath it. The projections below are TSS-only — Artha is a live, paid pilot but is not yet in this model (see below).

  • 1. B2C Thesis Support (The Base): Live with ~€300 MRR and 25,000+ active users, modelled to grow ~15% per quarter.
  • 2. B2B Universities (The Scale): Selling our Thesis Support System into universities across South Asia and the EU. ~4.5-month sales cycle. Pricing is regional: €30k ACV in the EU (Helsinki, Metropolia, TAMK and later EU logos), €12k ACV in South Asia (Pune University, Kathmandu University, NUST University — reflecting materially lower institutional IT budgets). No institutional logos are modelled to close before Sprint evidence lands in Q2 2027.
  • 3. B2B Artha (Not Yet Modelled): A specialised grant-writing module. Professors directly requested and paid us to build and pilot this tool. Active paid pilots with the City of Vantaa and Business Tampere, anchored by an active R&D LOI with Tampere University. Pricing tiers €2.5k–€20k. Artha carries cost today (AI Lead + engineering time) but no revenue line in these projections yet.

3. The Grand Finale (The KPIs)

2026 is a stub year (base date 28 Aug 2026) — burn stays lean and institutional revenue is pre-close. The first evidence-led institutional deals land in 2027 as Sprint validation and Seed T1 hires come online; growth compounds through 2028 into a fully ramped team.

  • Runway: The €1.5M Seed + €100k Sprint grant self-fund operations through to the Series A on their own — modelled company cash never drops below ~€736k (Q3 2028), even before Series A lands.
  • 2026 (stub, Q3–Q4): We exit the year at ~€4,800 ARR (B2C base only; no institutional logos have closed yet) and ~€10,100 recognised revenue.
  • 2027: Growth to ~€146,000 ARR and 7 institutional logos by year end, with Seed Tranche 2 landing Q2 2027.
  • 2028: ~€525,000 ARR and 26 institutional logos by year end, crossing the Series A bar (€500k+ ARR, 15+ logos) in time for a late-2028 raise.

Risk Assessment & Mitigation

Risk Area Mitigation Strategy
Long Sales Cycles (Universities) Revenue Diversification: Steady B2C revenue provides cash flow continuity while the institutional pipeline converts. Artha (departmental budget) offers a faster B2B sales cycle than full university procurements once it's brought into the model.
AI Commoditization Data Moat: Proprietary workflow data from students → researchers trains our models to be increasingly specific and defensible against generic LLMs.
Hiring Delays Phased Hiring: Seed tranche 1 (Q4 2026) funds 3 critical engineering hires from Q1 2027; tranche 2 (Q2 2027) unlocks the commercial team. Each phase is gated to milestones, reducing recruitment pressure.
Seed Closing Risk Multi-Phase Seed: Splitting into €500k T1 (Q4 '26) and €1M T2 (Q2 '27) reduces single-event risk. Sprint pilot evidence lands between tranches (project completes Mar '27) to strengthen the T2 close.
Sprint Delay Risk Single Point of Dependency: Every institutional close in this model is gated on Sprint evidence (Oct '26–Mar '27) and the 4.5-month sales cycle after it. A delayed Sprint pushes the entire institutional revenue line and the T2/Series A milestones with it.
Series A Risk Multi-Stage De-risking: Sprint funding validates South Asian institutional sales before the Seed; Seed proves traction before Series A. Each round is unlocked by hitting milestones from the previous one, and Seed + Sprint alone fund operations through to the Series A (cash never drops below ~€736k) — the round is a growth accelerant, not a runway necessity.
